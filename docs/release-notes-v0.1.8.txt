## Photo Enhancer v0.1.8 (beta)

Best Quality water erase now uses classical water exemplar instead of SDXL. Same coastal quality as v0.1.7’s SDXL+plaque_rescue path, without multi-GB VRAM thrash on 16 GB cards. PowerPaint (indoor/library) also offloads when free VRAM is under 10 GB.

### Prefer this over

- **v0.1.7** — water Best still loaded SDXL then rescued soft plaques
- **v0.1.6 and older** — packaging / coastal plaque issues

### Highlights

- Water / coastal Best → `water_exemplar` (no SDXL)
- PowerPaint `enable_model_cpu_offload` when free VRAM < 10 GB
- Retains v0.1.7 blobify / head-band coastal mask fixes

### Install (split download)

GitHub limits assets to 2 GB. Download **both** parts, then:

```powershell
cmd /c copy /b PhotoEnhancer-v0.1.8-windows-x64.part1.bin+PhotoEnhancer-v0.1.8-windows-x64.part2.bin PhotoEnhancer-v0.1.8-windows-x64.zip
Expand-Archive PhotoEnhancer-v0.1.8-windows-x64.zip -DestinationPath PhotoEnhancer-v0.1.8
cd PhotoEnhancer-v0.1.8
.\PhotoEnhancer.exe
```

Models download on first use to `%APPDATA%\PhotoEnhancer\models\`. Best Quality indoor scenes still benefit from PowerPaint (~6 GB) from the Models panel (NVIDIA CUDA). Coastal water no longer requires SDXL Inpaint.

### Requirements

- Windows 10/11 x64, 8 GB+ RAM, 10 GB+ disk (20 GB+ with Best Quality indoor)
- WebView2; internet only for model download
- Close other GPU apps before Best Quality PowerPaint on 16 GB cards
